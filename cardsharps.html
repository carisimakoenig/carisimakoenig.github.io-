The Cardsharps

Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio

Home Gallery Resources

Completed 1597. Acquired 1987.

In The Cardsharps, Caravaggio depicts two cheats conspiring against an unsuspecting young cardplayer. Through subtle gestures, expressive faces, and richly detailed clothing, he transforms an everyday scene into a study of deception and human behavior. Rediscovered after decades of obscurity, the painting was acquired by the Kimbell Art Museum in 1987 from a private European collection.
