The Musicians

Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio

Home Gallery Resources

Completed 1597. Acquired 1952.

Caravaggio depicts four young musicians preparing to perform, combining naturalistic observation with an allegory of music and love. Cupid appears among the figures, while the artist includes his own likeness. Painted for Cardinal Francesco Maria del Monte, this early work was acquired by the Metropolitan Museum of Art in 1952.
