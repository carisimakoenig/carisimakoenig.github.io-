The Denial of St Peter

Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio

Home Gallery Resources

Completed 1610. Acquired 1997.

Painted shortly before his death, Caravaggio’s The Denial of Saint Peter captures the apostle denying his association with Christ. Dramatic lighting illuminates Peter’s troubled expression as two figures confront him. Through restrained color, expressive gestures, and psychological intensity, Caravaggio explores guilt, fear, and remorse. The Metropolitan Museum acquired it in 1997 from a private collection.
