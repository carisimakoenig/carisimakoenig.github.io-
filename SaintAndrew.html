The Crucifixion of Saint Andrew

Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio

Home Gallery Resources

Completed 1607. Acquired 1976.

Caravaggio depicts Saint Andrew's martyrdom with striking realism, capturing the moment his executioners attempt to release him from the cross. Dramatic contrasts of light and shadow emphasize the saint's suffering and the surrounding figures' emotional responses. Painted in Naples, this remarkable work was acquired by the Cleveland Museum of Art in 1976 where it began as an altarpiece in the Spanish viceroy's palace in Naples.
